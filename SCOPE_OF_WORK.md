02 | OEM / Manufacturer Panel
• OEM/company profile management and authorized vendor/service partner onboarding.
• Product, equipment, model, brand and service-category master management.
• Warranty number, purchase date, warranty period, eligibility and status management.
• Create and track service requests against products/equipment and customers.
• Route calls to authorized vendors/service partners using brand, city, skill and certification rules.
• Monitor TAT/SLA, service status, escalations, repeat visits and closure evidence.
• Review/approve service closure and access service history, MIS, vendor performance and company-wise reports.

03 | Vendor / Service Company Owner Panel
• Register a service company by legal/company name, address, service area, KYC documents, GST/tax details where applicable and
authorized brands.
• Add, edit, deactivate and manage company engineers, their skills, certifications, availability and service territories.
• Assign local calls received by the company to its own engineers.
• Request an outside-location partner company or independent engineer when local capacity is unavailable.
• Clearly distinguish own-company calls, platform-assigned calls, outsourced calls and partner-company calls.
• Track call status, engineer location, eFSR, quality, call-wise payout and settlement.
• View engineer performance, attendance/day status, approved calls, rejected/repeat calls, earnings and payment disputes.
• Request reassignment/escalation and track outsourced job completion and accountability.
I COMPANY OWNER WORKFLOW
Owner registers the company → adds engineers → receives/assigns local calls → requests external coverage for other cities → monitors
service evidence, approval, engineer payout and company settlement.

04 | Engineer Mobile App & Servoshakti Tracker Rules
• Engineer registration, KYC, skills, certifications, brand authorization, availability and profile status.
• Receive, accept or reject service calls; show acceptance deadline and assignment status.
• Day Start is compulsory before the engineer can begin eligible field-service activity.
• No manual End Day button: the system automatically closes the engineer day at 11:59 PM IST.
• Engineer can view only their own assigned calls, work history, service evidence, day status, approved call count and payment status.
• Engineer manually enters CRM/SR reference and call details where required by the Servoshakti process.
• GPS check-in, location validation and distance calculation are recorded for the service visit.
• Capture equipment serial number, readings, work performed, remarks and before/after photographs.
• Customer OTP and/or signature is required for service closure according to configured job policy.
• Completed calls require report/photo evidence and eFSR submission.
• Admin/OEM/vendor verification is required before a call becomes eligible for payment approval.
• QG/Fuji report generation is not part of this platform scope; the existing system remains the record for those reports.
I ENGINEER DATA PRIVACY
An engineer must not see another engineer’s private call details, customer records, performance or payouts unless explicitly authorized
through a defined supervisory role.

06 | Customer Module & Product Passport
• Customer registration/login and profile, contact details and service address.
• Create a product passport after purchase with product brand, model, serial number, invoice, purchase date, warranty start/end and
supporting document/image.
• Validate required fields and allow customers to review/edit details before saving.
• Show product/service history, warranty status, active complaints, service visits and closure evidence.
• Allow a customer to initiate an AI-assisted complaint by text or voice, or use the standard manual service-request flow.
• Customer can track complaint/SR number, status, assigned service company/engineer, expected TAT and updates.
• Capture customer OTP/signature and feedback where required at service closure.
• Send configurable warranty-expiry reminders and service updates through enabled communication channels.
07 | AI Customer-Care Complaint Assistant (Phase 1B)
This is a distinct workflow from field-service execution. The AI assistant helps a customer register a complaint with the relevant brand/OEM
customer-care team and captures the reference details before any field-service dispatch, where applicable.
STEP
FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENT
1. Product passport
Customer selects a registered product or adds invoice, serial number, model, purchase date and warranty details.
2. AI intake
Customer describes the problem by text or voice; AI transcribes where applicable and creates a concise
complaint summary.
3. Customer confirmation
Show the complaint summary and ask the customer to confirm/edit before submission or outbound call.
4. AI brand call
AI calling service contacts the brand’s customer-care number, follows the configured call script and requests
complaint registration.
5. SR number and TAT
Capture service request/reference number, expected turnaround time, relevant notes and call outcome.
6. Human handoff
If the customer wants to speak to an executive, attempt a live call bridge/three-way connection where technically
supported and consented to.
7. Transcript/recording
Store call outcome and, where lawful and consented, transcript/recording with access control and retention policy.
8. Failure fallback
If AI call fails, queue retry, offer human/operator fallback, record failure reason and escalate based on configured
rules.
9. Follow-up
Schedule automatic follow-up/escalation when the SR is missing, TAT is exceeded or the brand has not updated
the complaint.
10. Warranty reminders
Send reminders before product warranty expiry using the customer’s saved product passport.
% AI CALLING SAFEGUARDS
AI calling, recording, transcription, call bridging and third-party voice services depend on provider capability, customer consent, applicable law
and provider charges. These integrations must be selected and validated before production.

11 | Rate Cards, Billing & Company-Wise Settlement
• Maintain a separate rate card for each OEM/company; the platform must not assume one universal rate card.
• Support brand-wise rates for preventive maintenance (PM), breakdown, installation and other configured job types.
• Support distance slabs and approved GPS/distance calculation rules.
• Distinguish warranty and non-warranty service rates.
• Store separate vendor payout, engineer payout and platform/service fee for each applicable call.
• Support different rates for the same engineer working under different OEMs/companies.
• Maintain call-wise rate snapshots so later rate-card changes do not silently rewrite historical settlements.
• Support company-wise invoice/MIS, P&L; visibility, settlement status and exportable reports.
• Provide configurable GST/tax fields and tax-reporting treatment according to the client’s approved accounting process; final tax configuration
requires client/accountant confirmation.
• Payment gateway/UPI integrations can be used where required; all transactions should have references, status and audit history.
I BILLING CONTROL
Payout approval should depend on approved service closure/eFSR and the applicable company rate card. Failed, rejected, duplicate and
repeat calls must follow separately configured payment rules.
12 | Smart Matching, OEM–Vendor Bridge & Control Tower
Smart Matching & OEM–Vendor Bridge
• Onboard OEMs/vendors and manage authorization, brand, product, service category and service-area mapping.
• Route warranty and non-warranty service work to authorized vendors and support outside-location requests.
• Track vendor performance, service history, job status and cross-location execution.
• Use location, equipment type, brand certification, skill, availability, radius and performance to match work.
Servoshakti Admin / Control Tower
• Manage OEMs, service companies, engineers, customers and partner relationships.
• Verify KYC, documents, certifications and authorization status.
• Monitor calls, matching, assignment, reassignment, escalations and service closure.
• Configure warranty, rate cards, service categories, radius, SLA/TAT, acceptance window and business rules.
• Monitor quality: TAT, first-time-fix, repeat complaints, ratings, customer approval and evidence completeness.
• Manage disputes, exceptions, wallet/recharge, payments, payouts and settlements.
• Access company-wise and city-wise MIS, service volume, warranty, SLA, payments, quality and P&L; reports.

14 | Backend, Security & Multi-Company Data Separation
• Centralized backend/API and database for all platform roles and modules.
• Role-based permissions for OEM, vendor owner, engineer, customer and Servoshakti administrator.
• Strict company-wise data separation: users only see the records their role and company are authorized to access.
• Secure storage and controlled access for KYC, invoices, product passports, photos, service reports, transcripts and recordings.
• Audit trails for important service, dispatch, AI-call, payment and administrative actions.
• Backup, retention, export and data-deletion processes must be defined before production.
• API-ready architecture for AI complaint intake, telephony/voice, messaging, maps/GPS, payment and external CRM/service integrations.
• AI calling, recording, transcription, three-way bridge and automated attendance/payment verification depend on approved external providers
and their technical capabilities.
I DATA GOVERNANCE
Client-owned domain, hosting, database/API accounts and repository access should be specified in the final development contract. Define
data export, backup frequency, retention, deletion and handover responsibilities before go-live.
